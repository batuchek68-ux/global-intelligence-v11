# Risk Assessment Framework

## Risk Levels

### Low (Score < 45)

- Reversible actions allowed
- No owner approval needed
- Standard documentation

### Medium (Score 45-74)

- Owner notification required
- Approval for external actions
- Enhanced documentation

### High (Score >= 75)

- Owner decision required
- All external actions blocked
- Full evidence dossier

## Risk Categories

### Sanction & Compliance

- Sanction screening
- Export control checks
- Compliance verification

### Financial Risk

- Payment route review
- Currency exposure
- Credit assessment

### Operational Risk

- Contract terms review
- Delivery timeline
- Quality assurance

### Political Risk

- Country stability
- Regulatory changes
- Trade policy shifts

## High-Risk Keywords

- sanction, export control, customs
- compliance, arbitration, claim
- breach, guarantee, payment
- political

## Approval Workflow

1. Risk assessment completed
2. Evidence dossier compiled
3. Owner notification sent
4. Decision recorded
5. Continuation plan created
